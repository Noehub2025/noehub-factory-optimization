# Consequential research decisions

Use [Evaluation, implementation and continuation](../../skills/frontier-optimization/references/learning-loop.md#evaluation-implementation-and-continuation) for actual evidence dependencies and scoped waiting. Fixed, noisy, delayed, evolving and qualitative evaluations do not share one mandatory delivery lifecycle. A pending observation neither locks all work nor requires constant activity. Compare truthful alternatives before applying an agent-invented guarantee or old fallback; reuse real user authority and preserve hard limits. Duplicate observations do not repeatedly reopen unchanged work.

Apply these principles to the next consequential investment or a material change to its mechanism or constraints. Ordinary implementation progress, file saves, tests and missing review metadata do not trigger another judgment. Use existing applicable professional judgment once. A concrete finding identifies the conflicting policy passage, decision passage and smallest useful correction; it does not certify originality or success.

Use [Whole-chain investment and target feedback](../../skills/frontier-optimization/references/learning-loop.md#whole-chain-investment-and-target-feedback) as the owner of feedback timing and evidence use. Judge the next investment from the objective sources, cumulative learning, gap and remaining path, not merely the latest local question. A candidate becoming capable of useful feedback, a material unresolved objective gap, changed conditions or further postponement makes timing due independently of whether feedback was selected before. Read the separately supplied objective/evaluation/current-work basis and compare a material omitted channel or its minimum prerequisite. Completing every selected local test does not establish adequate coverage. Resolve into observing, necessary preparation, an obtainable grant, real waiting, a better commitment with switching conditions, or retirement. Selected feedback runs when ready; optional polishing cannot postpone it. Pending observations retain an owner and actual delivery across handoffs. Worthwhile independent or bounded conditional work may proceed during waiting; no parallel task is required. A new core mechanism needs no prior gain, and feedback may support retaining the route. Use existing decisions and records.

- Recover the objective, gap and real limits from their original sources before assessing the proposed menu. A current architecture, local deliverable or earlier recommendation remains a working choice unless its necessity is established. Correct unsupported narrowing in the actual output task; acknowledging this policy is insufficient. A local anomaly may motivate a different organization without another similar failure first.
- Apply [Discovery allocation](../../skills/frontier-optimization/references/learning-loop.md#discovery-allocation) at direction formation, material reconsideration and ongoing-work recovery. Perform proportionate discovery beyond the supplied menu, reuse an applicable inquiry, explicitly defer it or preserve a real access/resource limit. Discovery needs an objective-relevant question, reachable evidence, an affordable action window and intended use; its method, final design and evaluation arrangement may be unknown. First entry, resumption and reuse for a continuing open objective retain or establish a reachable discovery point even when local work succeeds. Carry prior deferrals; repeated reasons alone do not discharge them. The linked rule permits a supported finite-window end of allocation while preserving uncertainty. A focused task needs no landscape review, and external prohibition means permitted evidence and an explicit visibility limit without renewed permission requests.
- Develop the intended organization and necessary interactions before shrinking its realization. Apply [Prospective reasoning](../../skills/frontier-optimization/references/learning-loop.md#prospective-reasoning) in this same decision: consider consequential external changes and intervention-induced effects, key combinations and delays, and opportunities for a different whole mechanism. Use them in the actual commitment and feedback arrangement, not a scenario checklist or a universal robustness objective. A whole idea can come from intuition, analogy or experience; proven superiority, a complete causal account and a preliminary successful scan are not admission requirements.
- Compare each serious alternative's next worthwhile commitment and unavoidable continuation at its present maturity. Alternatives need not answer the incumbent's internal question; compare what each can contribute to the objective. Include necessary coupled work and opportunity cost; do not charge one alternative for its eventual entire programme while charging another for one ready test. Reusable assets reduce remaining cost, but proximity to finishing an explanation, small size and readiness do not establish priority.
- An effective incumbent is a reference and reusable capability, not a compulsory architecture. A different mechanism need not wait for incumbent failure or preserve immediate fallback unless an actual requirement makes that necessary. An open objective does not itself authorize effects or spending outside existing limits.
- Carry the selected mechanism, necessary interactions and objective-related feedback into design and implementation. Internal steps may be ordinary development without independent gains or reviews. Necessary debugging can proceed within a still-worthwhile commitment. Support inherits that commitment's objective value; reducing implementation uncertainty alone does not justify a new investment. Estimate necessary dependencies before expanding support, without requiring a preliminary investigation or narrowing away the behavior the idea needs.
- At handoff, material steering, direct work, return and recovery, compare the actual action/result with the selected mechanism and objective basis under [Current-use correction](../../skills/frontier-optimization/references/worker-interfaces.md#current-use-correction). Carry due discovery and applicable known corrections across edits, renaming and owner transfer. Correct the affected current task and dependencies, explicitly supersede obsolete restrictions in follow-up, and inspect the next substantive work. Matching identifiers, `addressed` status or a `focused`/unchanged-investment label cannot excuse an actual new restriction. A confirmed limited-impact advisory and unrelated work retain their existing path. Saved adopted corrections may be uncommitted; historical evidence retains its original identity. Checks can establish use of the current object, not sound reasoning, and coverage claims must match the backend actually exercised.
- Valid objective feedback can guide continuation before causal attribution. Select explanatory or measurement support when its contribution to a worthwhile commitment justifies its remaining cost relative to other feasible next work, or complete it when explicitly requested. Missing evidence limits dependent actions and claims; it does not automatically select diagnosis. Preserve real evidence, validity and permission boundaries while allowing a different eligible action. Missing optional explanation neither invalidates an otherwise valid result nor erases adverse outcomes.
- Interpret a failure at the scope actually tested. Reject a wider mechanism only when the evidence bears on a shared necessary premise. At an existing investment trigger, reconsider weakened rationale, growing remaining cost or a better opportunity; an unrefuted hypothesis need not retain its priority. No fixed failure count, mandatory extra probe or automatic global reranking is added. For a concrete mismatch between a proposed capability and its evaluation arrangement, apply [Evaluation capability and next work](../../skills/frontier-optimization/references/learning-loop.md#evaluation-capability-and-next-work); missing competence neither erases the outcome nor automatically selects more development.

A focused answer, an ended research allocation and a direction decision have different scope. An early feasible option, completed document, locally sufficient evidence or lack of predicted novelty does not alone end direction discovery. Stop at actual resource/action bounds or when the remaining reachable inquiry has less value than acting, reasoning or probing now; retain consequential uncertainty and the next action. There is no fixed additional-round cap. Intermediate work inside the inquiry needs no new resolution, and selected useful feedback proceeds when ready without waiting for a survey.

Use the existing invocation's result contract. A normal resolver retains its substantive choice and any required `feedback_decision`; policy handling is not a replacement. A bridge check returns `no_finding`, `revise`, or `unavailable`, with a brief reason. Missing information or a failed checker is unavailable, never a pass or a research rejection. Correct or contest a finding through the existing owner once; no critic-of-critic loop. An owner may continue with a recorded supported disagreement. Real permission, resource, unsettled-effect and user-stop limits remain binding.
