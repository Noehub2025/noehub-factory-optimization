## Scope and separation

Use isolated temporary workspaces and preserve ordinary requests and raw inputs separately. The original source fixtures were disposable local evidence and are not installed dependencies or bundled sample files. Executors must not read this assessment guide or unrelated audit/test material. No installation, campaign mutation or external execution is authorized. Read-only professional inquiry is available. The local file consumer records a result without judging its meaning.

The first-use and sufficient-reuse executors were created with `fork_turns: none`; their assignments supply the normal design Skill and necessary raw material, without the missing practice, source name or expected verdict. Assess original host tool-call records and the actual output, not a retrospective sequence account. These samples do not estimate scientific competence or a causal improvement over the old Workflow.
