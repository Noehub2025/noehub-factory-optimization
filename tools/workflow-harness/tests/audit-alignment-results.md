# Consequential-meaning audit verification

This is an inherited source-verification record. The source executor samples
and independent reviews were not rerun during distribution migration.
Distribution checks are reported separately. Consuming-project code and business
state described below are outside this portable bundle.

Date: 2026-10-07. Scope: installed platform-neutral Workflow instructions,
independent review guidance, and isolated local behavior samples. This change
does not alter project goals, business allocation, experiment records or external
operations. The optional Codex Hook and production Python/Rust code are unchanged.

## Problems and implementation

| Problem | Implemented change | Verification boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WA-01: a technical choice gains user or environmental authority | Compare the claimed authority with its applicable original evidence; a file name, selector or agreement is insufficient. | Source comparison is professional judgment, not automatic authentication. |
| WA-02: local conditions expand or temporary choices become permanent | Inspect the actual configuration, message, command and interpretation; reconsideration conditions themselves can be challenged. | No expiry registry or review of every default. |
| WA-03: independent agents share one wrong premise | A selected checker can question an influential premise and derive its scope from original sources; ordinary continuation can be sampled. | A different agent or consistent files do not establish independent evidence. |
| WA-04: upward feedback disappears through deferral | The owner resolves, retains, investigates or refers the question; still-relevant deferrals survive recovery and reach the next affected decision. | Every suggestion need not receive investigation. |
| WA-05: a better question requires proof that the old one is false | Reuse existing opportunity proposals and investment ownership without requiring a successful replacement. | Actual user goals and uncovered effects remain user-owned. |
| WA-06: corrected prose does not change use | Adoption ends at the actual consumer or immediate exclusion, stop or final claim, including no-next-task cases. | No artificial rerun or new task solely to prove closure. |
| WA-07: audits displace research | Reuse existing checks, roles and records; selected samples have a reachable point and bounded question, but are not milestone gates. | No measured claim of net research-efficiency improvement. |

The normative addition is `worker-interfaces.md#Audit consequential meaning`.
Frame and Frontier entrypoints route to it; result adoption handles upward
questions; `review-frontier` permits scrutiny of the sampled premise. Source
delivery documentation and the Harness README distinguish consistency from
authority and name the limits of adapter coverage. There is no new Skill,
review kind, state schema, approval chain, runtime gate or per-assignment call.

The source audit retained exact pre-turn copies of its seven edited files in
a disposable local workspace. Earlier uncommitted work was not part of that
increment. The older header/body correspondence repair remains documented in
[restriction-drift-results.md](restriction-drift-results.md); it was neither
reimplemented nor counted as a newly discovered defect.

## Sample design and disclosure

[audit-alignment-cases.md](audit-alignment-cases.md) contains operator expectations
and reproduction instructions. `audit_alignment_samples.py` creates ordinary
workspaces in an absent or empty destination and refuses to overwrite contents.
The source audit used disposable local workspaces and retained unmodified
starting records separately. Run the supplied builder at a fresh destination
for new structural checks; reproducing executor judgment requires separate work.

Executors received their ordinary workspace assignment and the installed shared
rules, without the builder, operator expectations or other sample reports.
Existing agents from unrelated completed tasks were reused because the host
had refused another agent thread. They were separate actors, not fresh-context
blind trials. The independent reviewer and terminal adopter were different
agents. No new external agent service or host-limit workaround was used.

The consumers read the supplied configuration or disposition. They perform real
local arithmetic or publication and retain actual inputs; they do not diagnose,
correct, grade or simulate successful Workflow checks. Historical handoffs and
repeated deferrals are seeded material, not observed historical agent behavior.

## Observed behavior

### Independent review and terminal adoption

The reviewer traced the proposed closure through two retained handoffs to the
original request. It found that local preparation was permitted, while the
current owner, summary and decision incorrectly attributed a dictionary-only
limit to the user. It preserved the pilot's one correction and one regression
on four examples, and the real offline/no-external-service/no-purchase limits.
It wrote only `review.md` and did not execute the publisher or select a route.

A separate owner adopted the finding and ran the unchanged publisher once.
`final-disposition.json` and the sole publication event contain the corrected
`decision.json`: the base matcher remains provisional, the tested modification
is not adopted on this pilot, and broader local information construction stays
permitted and untested. The owner completed the bounded reporting assignment,
without declaring the broader research objective complete or running another
experiment. The next investment question remains explicit. Source request,
historical records and publisher bytes match the raw baseline.

### Direct recovery and actual configuration

The recovering owner changed the population configuration from a two-record
smoke-test limit to all supplied records. The unchanged consumer actually read
records 1 through 6 and produced count 6, sum 144 and mean 24. The separate
paired output kept its first two records and seed 7, producing mean difference
1.5. `events.jsonl` contains exactly two compute invocations, one for each output.

The owner resolved F1 in the configuration, actual output and current summary,
retaining previous deferrals as history. F2 retained its supported decision not
to investigate a configuration-format migration; no additional review or
format migration was started. The result describes the supplied records only.
The seed was consumed and preserved, but an order-insensitive mean does not
demonstrate the reproducibility of arbitrary stochastic experiments.

### First-pass recovery failure and repair

Both owners initially produced correct consumer outputs but left prospective
execution commands in current task/owner/summary files. A completed outcome at
the end did not unambiguously retire those commands. Independent review found
this in the direct-recovery sample; primary inspection found the same problem
in the terminal sample. This is a real sample closure failure, not a failed
scientific result or a reproduced production incident.

The original executors received scoped follow-ups and marked those instructions
as completed history at the recovery entries. They did not rerun either
consumer. Events remain one publication and two computations; adopted decisions,
configurations and outputs were unchanged by the status correction. Each outcome
retains the first-pass defect and follow-up repair. The shared rule now explicitly
requires retiring completed affected execution commands at the recovery entry.
These observations demonstrate review-assisted closure, not flawless autonomous
compliance; the final added sentence has not had a fresh blind behavior trial.
