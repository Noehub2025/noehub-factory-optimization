# Reflect Frontier

Act as the fresh-context owner of one Generation Reflection. Convert adopted results into a better starting point for the next search. Write technical search guidance, not campaign administration or the next formal Selection.

## Preconditions

Require:

- the canonical task path and generation number;
- the parent objective, real constraints, retained result, and remaining objective gap;
- every adopted terminal B and E for this generation, with their validity and comparison limits;
- the exact candidate, route, design, or representation lineage needed to understand behavior-bearing differences;
- relevant Q, X, bounds, failures, costs, and prior-generation Reflection;
- the established campaign-wide stop or halt and reconciled active-work state; and
- one exclusive path: `frontier/reflections/generation-<number>.md`.

Use adopted evidence only. Do not rerun candidates, checks, measurements, research, or reviews. Read identifiers only to reach the evidence; keep workflow status, authorization history, and artifact chronology out of the technical synthesis.

This step is ready when the evidence set covers the generation's material successes, failures, costs, retained search assets, and unresolved objective gap. Return the exact missing evidence if it does not.

## 1. Reconstruct the search outcome

Read the smallest complete evidence chain:

1. Start with the real objective, retained result, constraints, and measurement meaning.
2. For each decision-relevant result, connect the pre-work hypothesis, behavior-bearing difference, implementation validity, observed outcome, comparison validity, and cost.
3. Compare compatible results to find repeated successful directions, dead regions, useful near-failures, changing returns, and failures that did not express the intended idea.
4. Separate whole-solution evidence from component attribution. A trustworthy whole-package improvement is a search asset even when individual contribution is unknown. Buy component diagnosis only when different answers would change the next parent, mutation, representation, evaluation, or allocation.
5. Treat invalid or non-expressive execution as evidence about implementation, measurement, or representation—not as evidence that the technical route failed.

This step is complete when every material result has a supported search consequence or an explicit reason why it changes none.

## 2. Extract search advantage

Write a concise synthesis that updates the next search along the dimensions supported by evidence:

- **Search assets:** the retained whole solution, other useful elites or stepping stones, reusable capabilities, and structures worth preserving.
- **Proposal distribution:** parents to reuse, mutation or construction families to increase or decrease, useful mutation scale, protected structure, and regions to avoid.
- **Representation:** changes to what may vary, what stays fixed, how candidates are expressed, or what feedback is exposed when the current representation limits productive variation.
- **Evaluation effort:** which opportunities merit a cheap screen, progressive evaluation, full comparison, or no more evidence. Opportunity cost applies equally to diagnosis and direct improvement.
- **Escape behavior:** when shrinking gains, invalid candidates, weak ranking signals, or one exhausted lineage justify a broader mutation, recombination, another elite, restart, or representation change.

Preserve diversity only where a distinct lineage offers material ceiling, a stepping stone, or protection from a deceptive local optimum. Do not spread effort evenly across dominated alternatives.

When adopted evidence may change a durable parent premise, name the possible parent implication in the relevant search-advantage or opportunity prose. Cite the underlying adopted evidence, explain how the premise could change future search, and state the conclusion that the evidence does not yet support. Use the evidence chain already required for the Reflection; perform no separate parent audit. A new elite, routine success or failure, stronger confirmation, unknown component contribution, or route change within the current Frame remains a Frontier lesson unless the evidence challenges durable parent meaning.

This step is complete when a fresh optimizer can generate a materially better candidate set than it could from the raw results alone and any stated parent implication is grounded in cited adopted evidence rather than the Reflection itself.

## 3. Propose worthwhile opportunities

Propose only the few directions that remain technically live and worth another look. Include both exploitation and high-upside exploration when the evidence supports both. For each direction state:

- what to change;
- why material improvement is plausible;
- which advantage gained in this generation it uses;
- the cheapest direct observation that could test its promise; and
- the result that would make it unattractive.

These are opportunity proposals, not a mandatory order. State an overall exploration or exploitation tendency when useful, but do not allocate Budget, authorize work, choose the next B, or replace the integrated resolver. Preserve an unsupported but high-ceiling idea as a conditional opportunity with its required evidence; do not present speculation as an established mechanism.

This step is complete when every proposed direction can seed candidate generation and has a clear loss-of-attraction signal. Do not add a direction merely to fill a category.

## 4. Write the Generation Reflection

Write the assigned file with these co-located sections:

```markdown
---
type: Optimization Frontier Generation Reflection
status: complete
generation: <positive integer>
generated: { by: reflect-frontier/1, at: "<ISO-8601 datetime>" }
---

# Generation <number> Reflection

## Search outcome
<strongest supported result, important limit, and remaining objective gap>

## Search advantage
<retained assets and concrete updates to parents, proposal distribution, representation, evaluation effort, or escape behavior>

## Worthwhile opportunities
<a small evidence-grounded opportunity set using the five questions above>

## Search stance
<exploration/exploitation tendency, high-ceiling lineages to retain, and conditions that would change the stance>

## Retire or revisit
<dominated or exhausted regions, repeated work to stop, and observable reopening signals>

## Evidence
<direct paths and identities supporting the synthesis, including important exclusions>
```

Use natural technical prose rather than reproducing the headings of every source record. The Reflection is complete when reversing a cited result would change the corresponding search lesson, unsupported attribution is bounded, positive whole-solution evidence remains usable, each opportunity is actionable for candidate generation, and process status can be removed without losing the insight.

The Coordinator may request one consolidated correction for an evidence error, material omission, or search-inert process summary. Revise the same exclusive file once. Return the final path and a one-sentence statement of the search advantage. A possible parent implication is advisory: the next Entry may carry its cited adopted evidence into the existing integrated resolver, and only that resolver's existing row 4 can formally refer an affected rule to Framing. The Reflection neither modifies nor triggers Frame, delays closeout, or requires extra research or diagnosis. The Reflection creates no Selection, route verdict, Budget allocation, authority, review result, or claim.
