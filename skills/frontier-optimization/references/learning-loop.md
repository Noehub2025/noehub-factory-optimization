## Evidence consequence levels

- **Routine:** valid expected evidence resolves the addressed decision inside the unchanged route, measurement meaning, material allocation, stop rule, and claim limit. Retaining or replacing an incumbent under an already adopted comparison and retention rule may be routine; the word `baseline` does not decide the level. Apply R8 without extra diagnosis.
- **Diagnostic:** surprising, failed, ambiguous, validity-limited, or decision-limiting measurement evidence may justify a direct attempt, local check, focused Q, isolated prototype, or authorized diagnostic-only observation. Compare next investments under the single resolver below; no unique-dominance requirement applies.
- **Strategic:** evidence supports crossing a reviewed route or material-allocation boundary, or changing budget policy, a stop rule, irreversible behavior, or a claim limit. A comparator, reference, or incumbent change is strategic only when its actual consequence crosses one of those boundaries. Measurement-meaning and design changes use their owning specialized gates; add Replan only when the same change is also strategic. Dependent strategic spend needs sufficient evidence, any applicable V, and adopted fresh-context replan review.

## Active learning chain

Treat `completed`, `interrupted`, `failed`, and `blocked` as terminal B outcomes after Coordinator validation. `waiting_for_input` is a paused attempt. After a terminal result and every eligible E are adopted:

1. Reconcile the result packet, W state, artifacts, spend, reservations, recovery, E, and X. Preserve negative, failed, ambiguous, and invalid evidence.
2. Establish validity in this order: implementation, measurement, then comparison validity. Stop inference at the first unresolved layer.
3. Build one current evidence-state identity from adopted project facts.
4. Apply the integrated resolver exactly once for that evidence-state identity and persist its first applicable row in Selection.
5. Complete only the Q, V, join, Replan, or specialized gate selected by that row. If Selection chooses a row 7 or row 8 Q, record that E0 resolution before research begins. Adopt the normalized Q result as a new evidence state E1, return to step 3, and resolve E1 once.

This chain creates no intermediate interpretation stage, Outcome Reflection, coverage record, review, or authority. E0 and E1 are separate evidence-state identities, not competing resolutions of one state. The resolver reads adopted B, E, Q, X, T, W, D, V, R8, Budget, parent, validity, and gate facts directly. A normalized Q may carry material specialist provenance, conflicts, challenge results, and transfer limits; raw role bundles, worker returns, and unadopted challenger output never enter the resolver. For a safe parallel set, wait for every selected member's terminal outcome and one joined X before dependent allocation. Same-B working observations and fidelity repairs remain inside [Boundary-preserving continuation](batch-current.md#boundary-preserving-continuation) and do not run the resolver after each observation.

Validity asks whether the implementation and observation were produced and compared as defined. Applicability asks whether that valid evidence satisfies the existing parent conditions for a particular use. A valid initialization value, intermediate snapshot, or unknown proxy remains valid evidence with a narrow factual meaning; its unsupported stronger use is ineligible. Do not turn that limitation into unresolved measurement validity, a new gate, or an automatic row 6 diagnosis.

Research or diagnosis is justified only when its answer can change eligibility, route, allocation, stopping, promotion, or claim limit. A direct reversible attempt may be preferable to separate diagnosis. Apply routine R8 without extra research when no earlier resolver condition applies. Use specialized gates in addition to replan review: changed design needs design review and authorization; changed candidate needs implementation review; changed claim needs claim review; parent-scope conflict needs parent review.

## Evidence use

Use adopted evidence; do not rerun the candidate, evaluator, engineering checks, repository tests, or workflow tests. Do not create a snapshot or review merely to decide what valid existing evidence means.

Read the smallest complete evidence chain in this order:

1. **Current technical decision:** read the terminal B's `Decision hypothesis`, `Expected observation`, result, terminal outcome, related E, parent metrics, guardrails, and technical decision boundary.
2. **Originating mechanism:** when B evaluates, confirms, or reuses an executable candidate, follow the exact candidate identity to the originating T or W, the design or implementation B that changed behavior, the executable-difference description or source manifest, the adopted implementation review, and the engineering or behavior evidence. Use identity-bound lineage rather than topic similarity. For a reference measurement, administrative B, or work with no technical mechanism, record `not-applicable` instead of inventing one.
3. **Comparison meaning:** read the adopted evaluator, protocol, comparator, workload or data scope, uncertainty, and result direction from E and its result. Add prior E only when the comparability rule below permits the exact interpretation being made. Read lower-level measurement artifacts only when the adopted result does not already establish a decision-relevant property; never repeat the measurement.
4. **Decision meaning:** read the applicable replacement boundary, disqualifying evidence, known limits, and only those traces, route alternatives, or design surfaces already supported by persisted evidence. Budget, authority, R8, and gate records constrain the action; they do not change what the evidence observed.

Synthesize that chain rather than summarizing each record:

1. Keep the current B decision and the originating technical mechanism as separate layers. Judge the B hypothesis from its expected and observed result; use the complete lineage only for the supported mechanism grain.
2. Connect the evidence explicitly: what behavior-bearing difference was introduced, what implementation evidence shows that its pathways exist, and what valid outcome evidence supports at whole-package level.
3. Name component activity, necessity, dominance, mediation, or numerical contribution only at the grain supported by separating evidence. Otherwise keep the package conclusion and state the attribution limit.
4. Identify the defect, assumption, or local question that the evidence resolves. Do not keep a supported issue open merely because its internal contributions were not decomposed.
5. Admit a new conjecture only when it is grounded in observed evidence and can change the current allocation; label it untested.
6. State whether the measurement remains fit for the next concrete decision. A completed decision stays complete even when its comparator would be too coarse for a future successor decision.
7. Use the strongest bounded conclusion supported by the evidence rather than a gate label such as `admit`, `reject`, or `completed`.

Evidence use is complete when the resolver can distinguish what was observed, what remains unresolved, which actions remain technically live, and which inference limits apply without creating a separate narrative artifact.

## Hypothesis and mechanism interpretation

Judge the precommitted technical question as:

- `supported` when valid evidence matches the predicted observable effect within the named scope;
- `contradicted` when valid evidence violates it;
- `inconclusive` when validity, resolution, or coverage cannot answer it; and
- `not-applicable` for work with no technical hypothesis.

Support is scoped evidence, not universal proof. For a terminal B, recover the controlling decision hypothesis from that B. When the B evaluates, confirms, or reuses an existing candidate, recover the originating mechanism through its exact candidate, T or W, design or implementation B, and adopted implementation evidence; this mechanism context cannot replace the B-level decision. Preserve both layers, the expected observation, contradiction, and decision boundary. Implementation-only evidence may support implementation of the pathway but cannot support its performance effect. A valid whole-treatment comparison may support that the bounded package caused the observed local effect when the treatment is the only executable difference and evaluator, protocol, and comparison integrity remain fixed.

A candidate that violates an adopted design constraint directly establishes that candidate's nonconformance; the possibility that the design may also be wrong does not invalidate the design. Record a design consequence only when valid evidence under the same relevant conditions is incompatible with a named feasibility rationale that supported `DESIGN_READY`. The evidence need not have been produced specifically to test the design.

When several explanations remain, record a missing distinguishing observation only if those explanations lead to different next concrete actions. Otherwise keep the ambiguity as an attribution limit or non-blocking note. Ambiguity alone creates no diagnosis, research, review, blocker, or spend; apply the single integrated resolver to the persisted evidence.

Infer mechanisms at the strongest supported grain:

- **Whole package:** a valid controlled comparison plus exact executable differences can support the package-level causal effect without ablation.
- **Component present:** source and behavior evidence can show that a component pathway exists and behaves as designed.
- **Component contribution:** necessity, dominance, mediation, or numerical contribution needs a separating intervention, ablation, trace, or equivalent evidence.

An unexplained component contribution limits attribution but does not invalidate a valid package-level result or block another bounded reversible attempt. Diagnose it only when the pending decision depends on choosing among those internal explanations. A new conjecture may be grounded in observations, known limits, or mechanism reasoning; label it untested, explain why it could matter and what observation could refute it. Evidence supports the reason to test, not the conjecture's truth. A conjecture grants no research, reuse, incumbent, or spend authority.

Interpret progress only when cross-B history can change the decision. Combine only E with compatible parent objective, measurement meaning, comparator role, protocol, workload, data scope, uncertainty, validity, and result direction, or an explicit equivalence argument. Apply the complete parent-owned vector. A single departure is not persistence; movement within noise or resolution is not a plateau; a bounded plateau or underperformance finding needs a prospective rule. A proxy cannot establish route progress while a required metric, hard constraint, guardrail, segment, tail, delayed confirmation, or operating-cost boundary fails or remains unresolved.

Use Constraint inference only when it affects the decision. `suspected` identifies a plausible limiting factor without system-level effect evidence. `demonstrated` needs an intervention or discriminating test showing that changing the factor changes the parent objective under the named conditions. `shifted` needs evidence that the intervention changed which factor limits that objective. Local difficulty, eliminated alternatives, or movement in a proxy does not establish a bottleneck.

Use a measurement limitation only when it changes a concrete decision. Distinguish a saturated comparator-derived score from exhaustion of the evaluator itself. A limitation that does not prevent the addressed decision remains a future-use note; it creates no protocol-repair requirement. If the limitation prevents the current decision, keep the evidence inconclusive and let the resolver compare a direct candidate attempt, direct incumbent comparison, protocol adjustment, diagnosis, research, or stop by decision information and cost.

Selection records the resolver's evidence-bound decision, diagnostic ordering, first applicable row, affected scope, surviving authority, and exact next action. R8, Budget, Replan, and specialized gates are inputs to that one resolver, not later override systems.

## Integrated direction resolver

This section is the only direction resolver. `frontier-core.md` owns the recorded-state router and the existing stop, halt, parent, and closeout actions to which rows below refer; it does not own a second direction table. `campaign-cycle.md`, `campaign-state.md`, Entry review, Replan review, and later-spend checks cite this section and never restate or reorder it.

### Opportunity-led reconsideration

New adopted evidence or a mechanism argument grounded in current project evidence can reopen a within-parent-scope route comparison when it could change allocation. Examples include newly reachable representations, transferable capabilities, or narrowing headroom in the current method. Record the basis in the existing Q, Entry route evidence, prior-generation Reflection, or current route-set state. An incumbent need not fail first. Mere novelty or another conceivable option does not require a refresh.

Compare the opportunity under [Route-investment ordering](#route-investment-ordering), including whether bounded research is worth doing. A concern may justify comparison without requiring research or a change of action. Route the selected commitment through the first applicable row below; earlier validity and authority conditions still apply. For opportunities outside parent scope, use [Opportunity proposals](frontier-core.md#opportunity-proposals) rather than treating a suggestion as a parent defect.

### Scoped evidence reuse

For retained research used in today's decision, compare its original decision and stopping premise with the pending commitment, objective, relevant Representation assumptions, operating conditions, resources, and new evidence. Reuse compatible observations, mechanisms, failed tests, and transfer limits. Reconsider only rankings, exclusions, sufficiency, or search-stop judgments whose supporting premise changed. Adoption preserves a judgment's provenance; it does not make the judgment an observation or permanently settle its applicability.

When the observation that justified an earlier research stop has arrived, that stopping premise no longer settles today's investment; compare the current choices. A new Generation, elapsed time, or workflow release alone requires no refresh. Revisit a negative search only when evidence, reachability, or the decision gives a reason. Use existing rationale and source references, not per-record reclassification, new version counters, or copied evidence.

Apply a threshold to route selection, stopping, or `technical no-path` only through its current normative owner and allowed consequence under [Decision-bearing thresholds](frontier-core.md#decision-bearing-thresholds). A repeated historical value, copied Entry predicate, Batch operational limit, regression line, diagnostic, or unsupported engineering target has no broader force. When workload, conditions, scope, statistic, direction, or consequence changed, return only the dependent judgment to the existing owner. A derived mismatch selects same-B repair; an unresolved threshold preserves the observation and every route or action that does not depend on it.

Before extending an implementation failure beyond the observed realization, apply [Scope of negative implementation evidence](frontier-core.md#scope-of-negative-implementation-evidence). A finding label, threshold ceiling, repeated trial of the same implementation shape, or absence of a tried alternative does not establish broader coverage.

### Resolver inputs and Budget precheck

Reconstruct one immutable evidence-state identity from the current project decision root, parents and handoff, hard authority facts, adopted terminal outcomes and E dispositions, joins, route set and reopening events, adopted normalized Q and V records, T replacement and checkpoint boundaries, R8, Budget and protected reserve, decision-critical context and action window, and every specialized gate. A prior-generation Reflection may supply opportunity proposals to Entry, but it is not a validity or authority source. Conversation, raw research returns, workflow implementation bytes, a release change, or a preferred answer is not an input.

Every stop consequence has one exact scope. A `candidate` stop ends the affected use of one immutable Candidate Revision while preserving the B when it still pursues the same independently judged result, together with the unchanged route, W design, Budget, and planning authority. A `route` stop closes one exact T or route allocation while preserving explicitly named surviving routes and authority. A `campaign` stop ends all campaign spend authority and enters full closeout. Select the smallest scope established by the evidence and precommitted R8 rule. A review verdict does not supply scope by its label: map its findings and maximum supported consequence through R8 and [Scope of negative implementation evidence](frontier-core.md#scope-of-negative-implementation-evidence). Map `IMPLEMENTATION_REPAIR_REQUIRED` to candidate scope when the findings establish only that the exact reviewed Candidate Revision fails its unchanged design and leave the independently judged result, route, design contract, allocation, and parent authority usable. Continue that result in the same B with a new Candidate Revision; preserve the prior revision, Review, result, and any published artifact. Apply a new Review, Permission, or charge only when the repaired revision's next actual Consequence and the parent charging rule require it. Create another B only for another independently judged result. A pre-publication engineering-check failure remains ordinary same-B work under [Boundary-preserving continuation](batch-current.md#boundary-preserving-continuation). A missing or conflicting scope is an owning-contract blocker, not a campaign stop.

Before applying row 5, identify the next decision-relevant observation for each still-live unresolved condition under [Informative checkpoints](planning-records.md#informative-checkpoints). It is sufficient when its possible results can change the next allocation, falsify a live explanation, tighten a relevant bound, or determine whether a larger test is warranted; it need not settle the entire research question. Record considered alternatives, what they distinguish, result-to-action branches, governed cost, opportunity cost, latency, dependencies, authority, action window, Budget and reserve effect, and reachability in existing Entry or Selection fields. Fund the next observation plus unavoidable follow-on commitments and safe stopping or recovery. Unknown downstream optional research cost does not block this stage; unknown cost of the authorized stage or unavoidable commitments does.

Honor any explicit R8 ordering and exclude dominated or unreachable alternatives. Unique dominance is sufficient, not necessary, for a technical choice. For validity and local diagnostics under rows 6 and 9, the Coordinator records a justified ordering in the existing operational diagnostic fields using objective upside, ability to distinguish live explanations, full cost, and opportunity cost. Apply the invocation and determined-action exemption below before treating route research as unnecessary. When eligible diagnostic options are indistinguishable, record that fact and a stable operational order without claiming technical superiority. Ask the user only for a genuine unresolved user-owned value, resource, or authority choice. Technical estimates, local runtime, internal operation counts and Batch operational limits remain Coordinator decisions unless they cross an explicit user boundary.

### Route-investment ordering

For an unresolved route-investment choice or a grounded challenge to its current evidence basis, invoke one independent resolver in fresh context inside this canonical run, before ruling out rows 7–8. Its applicability assessment, technical order, and first applicable row form one resolution. It receives the adopted inputs above, including retained judgments with their scope, and is the sole author of this technical order. The Coordinator checks factual applicability, identity, completeness, Budget, authority, action window, and existing gates, then records the order unchanged in Selection or returns the exact contract defect. Technical disagreement is not a contract defect. This delegation creates no second table, result identity, approval, or authority.

When applicable R8 rules and adopted facts, or a still-applicable adopted resolver decision, already determine an executable next action and no grounded challenge remains, use the normal Coordinator path without a comparative pass. This includes a changed action fixed by a newly satisfied positive, directly checkable precommitted switch. A fallback conditioned on the absence of another worthwhile action, including `otherwise preserve`, does not establish its own premise and cannot use this exemption. Apply the existing rule to its facts; no new precommitment record or certificate is needed. A fresh Coordinator preference or unsupported completeness assertion cannot establish this exemption. A preferred answer alone is not a reopening signal.

Compare commitments within the user's actual scope. A planning-only grant can include permitted research and mechanism analysis; narrowing the choice to which design to write must not silently exclude them. A task label or zero metered charge does not choose the action class or authorize access beyond that scope.

The independent resolver applies this process:

1. Read the pending decision and relevant evidence changes. Separate observed facts and hard constraints from retained hypotheses, rankings, exclusions, and stopping judgments under [Scoped evidence reuse](#scoped-evidence-reuse). Remove only illegal, unauthorized, unreachable, out-of-scope, or currently unaffordable commitments. Novelty, unfamiliarity, early evidence, and optional later cost do not establish ineligibility.
2. Compare evidence at the route's current decision maturity: `admission`, `screen`, `validation`, `scale`, or `transition`. Admission needs a credible distinct mechanism, material possible upside, and a reachable evidence path; it does not need final performance or reuse proof. Also admit bounded landscape inquiry when a problem-grounded mechanism, coverage, shared-assumption, or Representation concern has a reachable evidence path and plausible findings that would change a commitment, order, stop, or switch. The answer, a replacement route, or one missing factual answer need not be known in advance. Notice and compare that concern from supplied evidence; professional causal mapping and retrieval belong to the selected Q. Maturity is a local comparison attribute, not persisted lifecycle state.
3. Identify each route's minimum sufficient commitment: the scale, duration, fidelity, Representation, prerequisites, and unavoidable follow-up needed to activate, observe, or distinguish its mechanism. Remove an underpowered probe rather than the route it fails to test.
4. Compare information acquisition and direct work by attainable objective change, credible bound impact, decision distinction, timing and option expiry, full cost and opportunity cost, unavoidable follow-up, downside boundary, reversibility, complementarity with retained routes, and reusable evidence. Include, when live, work that clarifies the target or scoring mechanism, tests proxy-to-objective linkage, measures or improves the objective directly, remains worthwhile across the current decision-relevant interpretations, or does not depend on the measurement gap. A proxy-dependent strong action is ineligible while its target relationship or required lifecycle context is unsupported, but that local gap does not exclude the other commitments. Full cost includes current human/agent effort and delay even when metered spend is zero; optional later work is not a current commitment. Explain the decisive comparison and its limit without inventing probabilities or numerical utility. Reversibility changes consequence evidence; it is not an automatic preference.
5. Require the incumbent to justify the marginal value of its next commitment; prior success and sunk cost add no merit. A wider route can win while the incumbent still works when mechanism, upside, and sufficient evidence path justify it; novelty alone cannot win. Identify shared high-consequence assumptions before counting their descendants as diverse routes, and scope unresolved prerequisites to work that depends on them. A frame challenge competes with direct progress only when it can change allocation; neither research nor direct progress must establish a globally best route.
6. Return `ordered_feasible_commitments`, one `selected_investment`, `missing_directions`, `preserved_routes`, evidence-established `exclusions`, and a result-to-action `switch_map`. Support switching on adverse evidence or on credible opportunity evidence such as larger upside, a cheaper sufficient test, a newly reachable capability, or an expiring option. These are alternative reasons, not mandatory fields for every route. Each selected action and switch names its existing Selection, B planning, X, parent, Replan, Entry, or specialized-gate surface and accountable owner. `transition` is a maturity label only; express its consequence as `deepen`, `scale`, `refer_parent`, `defer`, or `stop` through an existing surface.

Use a deterministic operational order when the remaining sufficient commitments are technically indistinguishable and state that no technical superiority was established. Selection consumes the resolver's complete order; it does not invent or replace a second ranking. Route-investment ordering is complete only when every live route is ordered, preserved with a reopening event, or excluded by a hard or evidence-established reason, and every decision-changing result has an existing destination and switching condition. If comparison retains ordinary continuation, consume that result through row 13 when no earlier row governs; comparison alone requires no Q, reallocation, or second call.

`complete for this decision` supports only the allocation that used it. When the last named commitment ends, assess the current action space from the objective gap, the new result, and still-applicable route evidence; this assessment is direction resolution, not automatic research. A remaining objective gap, an exhausted route list, an unnamed target, or inability to prove global optimality does not by itself select row 7. Select row 7 only for a concrete problem-grounded concern with a reachable evidence path and materially different findings that change recorded actions. If neither a worthwhile direct action nor such a concern remains, select a campaign stop and full closeout. `preserve_frontier` describes retention after a user pause or closeout; it is not a selected investment for a running campaign.

Do not ask the user to choose a technical diagnostic. Treat a proposed observation as the same proxy diagnostic class when it uses the same H measurement for the same unresolved target relationship and pending investment question. Before another such investment, require new evidence that materially changes the possible result-to-action branches or its expected decisiveness; a new record, evidence-state identity, or probe name alone is insufficient. Repetitions, samples, fixed schedules and confirmation already included in the selected commitment remain part of that commitment. Do not require the completed observation to have produced the hoped-for distinction when it was prospectively sufficient.

### Research before a formal direction choice

A formal direction choice may receive one additional evidence-completion round only through row 7 or row 8. Row 7 uses the route-landscape branch of [`research-frontier`](../../research-frontier/SKILL.md); row 8 uses its direct focused-question branch. Evidence channels are assigned only when they can change the named decision. The adopted Q result contains the applicable project, academic, industrial, community-lead, retained-result, failure, Reflection-opportunity, and mechanism evidence with provenance, operating conditions, conflicts, negative findings, and transfer limits.

The research target must name the allocation or explanation decision it can change, materially different possible findings and their different recorded actions, why a cheaper local observation cannot answer it, the latest useful return point, assigned evidence channels, and a fixed search stop. These findings are prospective branches, not known answers. Information is sufficient when it supports a justified next commitment or sufficient observation and further retrieval is unlikely to change that allocation under the available Budget, authority, and action window. Remaining unknowns retain their scope; sufficiency does not mean exhaustive coverage, proof of originality, or certainty that no better route exists.

Selection records the E0 row 7 or row 8 resolution before Q begins. An in-run revision of a provisional completeness assessment is part of that one resolution, not another E0 decision. After the normalized Q result is adopted, rebuild E1 and run this same resolver once for E1. When it records a determined direction, including the independent resolver's order when applicable, proceed through the named existing B, X, parent, Replan, Entry, or specialized-gate surface. Research that finds no superior route does not trigger another Q: further research needs a materially different decision-relevant basis or evidence path. Preserve uncertainty while continuing useful permitted work. If a real prerequisite remains unresolved, pause only its dependent action and retain other legal choices; missing prose or uncertainty about global optimality creates no new blocker.

## Historical compatibility

Keep every earlier Outcome Reflection immutable under the project evidence and workflow that produced it. It remains usable within its original evidence boundary, but current active generations require no new per-B OR and no OR coverage migration.
