# Strategic replan review

Load only for an actual strategic change under the [single direction resolver](learning-loop.md#evidence-consequence-levels). Internal revision allowances, development stopping conditions and support repair inside the same investment use [Batch continuation](batch-current.md#repair-an-existing-execution-restriction), including when an earlier X called them strategic. The Coordinator corrects that routing before assigning Replan review; the document containing a limit does not determine its ownership. This file adds no resolver, interpretation stage, or user authorization.

Apply [Change impact and retained results](frontier-core.md#change-impact-and-retained-results) when parent rules or retained conclusions are involved. A parent version difference alone does not require Replan; an actual strategic allocation change still requires `REPLAN_READY` before dependent spend. Review the change and affected dependencies, reusing unaffected conclusions.

## Saved review subject

Use the current [review-frontier interface](../../review-frontier/SKILL.md): one saved Git commit, explicit subject paths, applicable parents, an assigned R path and a completion condition. Keep the proposed strategic change in the existing X. Reference the current Budget, Selection, B, V and evidence owners instead of copying them into another packet. The saved proposal states which owning fields would change and why; it does not rewrite unrelated state.

Resolve saved references through [Provenance and Git](provenance-and-identity.md#current-reference-tools). Include the adopted triggering evidence, applicable saved resolver result, proposed allocation and affected design, measurement or claim conclusions. Retrieve additional source paths only when needed for that change. Historical packets and roots remain readable at their original versions; current review preparation does not regenerate them.

## Strategic replan review method

Before writing a verdict, the reviewer must:

1. verify the saved project subject, applicable parent rules and proposed change; workflow files and deployment versions are not project review inputs;
2. reconstruct the relevant adopted evidence from its B, E, Q, X and W sources, rather than accepting copied summaries as new authority;
3. check implementation, measurement and comparison validity for the proposed consequence;
4. verify the hypothesis, observed result, supported mechanism grain, attribution limit and applicable parent result vector;
5. check considered alternatives, result-to-action branches, full cost, reachability and investment rationale under the single resolver; for a measurement-dependent switch, apply [Evidence sufficient for the decision](../../frame-optimization/references/measurement-design.md#evidence-sufficient-for-the-decision). Return a material meaning or fitness change to the existing measurement owner. Reject a dominated repeat or a changed persisted choice, not technical discretion among eligible alternatives;
6. verify that the proposed Selection follows its saved first applicable resolver row, affected scope, surviving authority and later-spend gates; do not run another resolver for the same evidence state;
7. require research only when the applicable resolver selects it and it can inform the current allocation in time;
8. check technical eligibility before a user decision and preserve the user's actual choice;
9. reconcile the proposed Budget, reserve, Selection and B changes through their owners. Internal engineering caps do not become strategic or user-owned merely because a previous X contained them. Reject routine use of protected reserve and dependent strategic spend before applicable adopted `REPLAN_READY`;
10. preserve separate design, implementation, measurement, claim and parent responsibilities without letting a specialized review choose a different route.

Return exactly `REPLAN_READY`, `REPLAN_REPAIR_REQUIRED`, `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED`, `PARENT_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, or `BLOCKED`. A positive result has no finding; every nonpositive result identifies the observed defect, affected consequence, source evidence and required correction.

## Review and adoption

Only `review-frontier` writes the assigned R, using its current artifact requirements. Cite the saved subject and affected source paths for checked conclusions and findings; completion means every applicable requirement above has an evidence-backed disposition.

The Coordinator adopts an applicable positive R and applies only the reviewed strategic changes at their existing owners. Where a B references that R, use [Maintained Batch operations](batch-current.md#maintained-batch-operations). A nonpositive or inapplicable review does not apply the proposed strategic allocation. Historical subjects and verdicts remain unchanged; unrelated current facts and work do not need to be rewritten.
