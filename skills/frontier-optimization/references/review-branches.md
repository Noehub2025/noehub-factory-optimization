# Frontier review branches

Select exactly the review kind named by the packet. Read its method and the cited project evidence. Supporting references are conditional, not a common reading bundle.

| Kind | Method | Read additional material only when |
|---|---|---|
| `entry` | [Entry review](entry-review.md) | Permission needs [User decisions](user-decisions.md), or the action needs [Batch continuation](batch-interface.md), a [technical design](technical-design.md) judgment, or a new [direction resolution](learning-loop.md). Inspect only cited W concerns. |
| `replan` | [Replan review](replan-review.md) | Its allocation depends on the [single resolver](learning-loop.md); specialized verdicts are inputs, not new review branches. |
| `design` | [Design review](design-review.md) | A named design concern or [professional assignment rule](technical-design.md) is relevant. |
| `implementation` | [Implementation review](implementation-review.md) | Publication or retry eligibility needs [Batch continuation](batch-interface.md) or [Candidate lifecycle](candidate-lifecycle.md). |
| `claims` | [Claim review](claim-review.md) | The claim depends on a specific [Reflection](learning-loop.md) or [claim record](claim-records.md). |

Read [Finding effects](finding-effects.md) when judging findings. Read a record template only when its meaning is genuinely needed; reviewers do not create Coordinator records.

Verify the fixed project subject and the applicable parents once through the existing provenance interface. Judge evidence completeness rather than reconstructing unrelated operational history. If another branch's verdict is cited, inspect that verdict and its applicability, not its whole review procedure. A missing necessary input blocks only its dependent conclusion.

The review is complete when the selected method has evidence-backed conclusions and its report identifies the exact subject, findings, maximum supported consequence and conditions.
