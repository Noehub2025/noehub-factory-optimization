# Fresh-context Reflection Analysis

Use this read-only reasoning pass after an adopted terminal result that bears on a technical hypothesis, mechanism, performance, representation, constraint, or bound. It interprets existing evidence before the Coordinator runs the direction resolver. It creates no worker assignment, campaign record, identity, review, authority, research task, test, experiment, or spend.

## Start in a fresh context

Dispatch one analyst with no inherited conversation. Give it only the smallest technical evidence set needed to follow the research interpretation in [Build the evidence chain](learning-loop.md#build-the-evidence-chain):

- the terminal B decision hypothesis, expected observation, result, terminal outcome, and related E;
- the exact candidate lineage through its originating T or W, design or implementation B, behavior-bearing difference, implementation review, and engineering or behavior evidence;
- the evaluator, protocol, reference, comparison-validity evidence, uncertainty, and compatible prior E with their controlling reflections; and
- the parent objective, technical constraints, replacement boundary, and already-adopted technical exclusions needed to interpret the result.

Point to exact records or named technical sections rather than supplying a Coordinator summary. Keep the current Selection, Budget, authority, R8 resolution, resolver result, campaign stop, closeout, and proposed next action outside the assignment. The analyst reads only the assigned evidence and writes no file.

## Produce the research interpretation

Before reading the calibration contrasts below, draft the final candidate content for:

- `Decision addressed`;
- `Technical hypothesis` and `Hypothesis result`;
- implementation, measurement, and comparison validity;
- `Mechanism inference` and `Attribution limit`;
- `R&D implication`;
- applicable measurement, progress, and constraint interpretations;
- `Maximum supported conclusion` and `Claim boundary`; and
- technical `Decision-relevant unknowns`.

Recover both the immediate B hypothesis and the originating mechanism when an executable candidate is evaluated, confirmed, or reused. Explain what observation the mechanism predicted, what was observed, and which previously live uncertainty the result strengthens, weakens, resolves, or leaves unchanged. Separate a whole-package effect, evidence that a pathway exists, and evidence that a component contributes. Insufficient component separation limits attribution; it does not erase valid package-level or implementation learning.

State the research allocation implication independently of current permission: what later work should preserve, which resolved question should stop consuming attention, and which technical uncertainty remains live. This creates no recommendation to spend and no authority.

Then read [Reflection calibration contrasts](reflection-calibration.md) and revise the draft once where the current evidence calls for it. The final interpretation is complete when:

- reversing the observed result would change the hypothesis judgment or technical information gained;
- losing comparison validity would remove any performance conclusion that depends on it;
- removing campaign process state would leave the research interpretation intact;
- each conclusion cites exact assigned evidence at its supported grain; and
- the text explains the information gained rather than merely restating the result or admission label.

Return the final research fields and their evidence citations to the Coordinator. The Coordinator may remove or narrow an unsupported claim or request one evidence-specific correction. It fixes the accepted research content before restoring operational context and applying the integrated resolver.
