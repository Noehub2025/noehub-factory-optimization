# Frontier Worker Interfaces

Give the worker the current research problem, why the work was selected, relevant facts and evidence limits, open questions and the local result it owns through existing source references. Keep an unsupported route conclusion as a hypothesis, not an assignment premise. Professional workers may correct a handoff premise and suggest plausible unverified technical opportunities in existing progress or the return, without first proving superiority. Follow [When to reconsider investment](learning-loop.md#when-to-reconsider-investment); ordinary local problems stay with their owner. This grants no wider write scope or allocation authority.

For frontier-seeking research and design, carry the core-mechanism question under [Technical potential](learning-loop.md#technical-potential), the decisive question and the observation that could change the judgment into the existing assignment. Separate actual user, effect and resource limits from tentative methods. A support-cost limit does not ban a technical capability by name. Minimal edits, baseline preservation and a chosen wrapper are not default design boundaries: permit necessary restructuring and coordinated changes within the selected commitment and write ownership. Correct an inherited restriction at its owner and current use, without rewriting historical evidence or adding an approval step.

Load this file when assigning work or undertaking simple implementation directly. It defines working responsibility, exclusive active writes and the small research and user-decision packets. Batch and review branches have separate action files.

## Permissions

For a changed wait arrangement, actual dispatch/follow-up, other steering, idle return or resumed work, apply [Evaluation, implementation and continuation](learning-loop.md#evaluation-implementation-and-continuation). The Coordinator invokes its applicable current-source check and checks the actual message or direct work, even without native capture. A worker's local wait does not suspend the whole effort; retain the observation owner and scoped dependencies. Clear work does not wait for Reflection, and duplicate observations need not interrupt an unchanged commitment.

The Coordinator names the responsible worker and exact writable files or sections. Keep one active writer per section; input or Candidate Revision paths do not imply write authority. Roles below describe the responsibility being performed, not permanent Agent identities. For simple implementation requiring no independence, one Agent may sequentially coordinate and implement under the same working rules, without a role-switch record or authorization. It may not overwrite another worker's evidence or replace an independent reviewer.

### Evidence access

Assignment references are starting points, not a read whitelist. Within the current question and actual access and resource limits, follow relevant source, dependency, original-record and official-document links. Local evidence may settle a focused fact; that sufficiency does not end a direction-level inquiry under [Discovery allocation](learning-loop.md#discovery-allocation). Source versions establish applicability, not a prohibition on consulting other versions; keep their meanings distinct. Such fact-checking and simple calculations belong to the assigned work, without another Q, B, approval or access log. Coordinate a materially wider research question or commitment with the Coordinator.

Preserve explicit user and sensitive-access limits, the reviewer's assigned judgment, the resolver's research boundary, and purposeful staged isolation such as measurement revision's independent reconstruction. Reading permission grants neither execution nor writes. Correct an internal read restriction that obstructs relevant evidence under [Constrain effects, not convenient forms](technical-design.md#constrain-effects-not-convenient-forms); do not ask for permission on each lookup.

### Write and adoption ownership

Every current worker assignment cites the owning B, R, V, W, or other human handles plus only the full Git commits, repository-relative paths, external artifact references, limits, and completion condition needed by that worker. It does not create or recompute decision, attestation, authority, execution, or outcome roots. Workflow source, Skills, validators, releases, and deployment paths are not project inputs. Historical typed chains and `workflow_source_binding` remain readable only for retained records that already use them.

Prepare W references under [Work Plan](work-plan.md#saved-design-references) and resolver inputs under [Learning Loop](learning-loop.md#resolver-input-preparation). Pass generated bindings as structured data or write them into the existing assignment with the tool. A textual dispatch names that assignment's location; it does not transcribe digests. The receiver reads the binding rather than recreating it from prose.

Before selecting a review kind or allocating an R, apply [Assurance by consequence](batch-evaluation.md#assurance-by-consequence). Express the selected question in the existing assignment; for example: "The prior R established input attribution and aggregation. The changed recovery path must preserve that attribution after interruption. Inspect the changed writer and affected reader; completion is evidence that this use is reliable, with any remaining prerequisite named." This is an example, not required wording or new fields. Prepare references under [Review input roles and corrections](provenance-and-identity.md#review-input-roles-and-corrections). Correct a later reference or method mismatch in this task, retaining completed work; the correction needs no new approval or re-dispatch cycle.

## Professional output and workflow decisions

### Reuse understanding and deliver the actual input

Keep current understanding in its existing Brief, working plan or professional explanation. Reuse applicable reasoning across handoffs. At a substantive return or adoption, update only meaning that changed: its evidence, conditions, contradictions, open alternatives and next missing observation. An unchanged result, new recipient or evicted view requires no rewrite or model-based freshness check. Workers propose a delta within their existing write scope; the existing owner adopts shared meaning once. A useful account does not end discovery, transfer an old ranking to a different question or make an interpretation authoritative.

Prepare the input for the actual responsibility. Include the original objective separately from local scope, applicable understanding, consequential unadopted results and necessary operational facts. Use source references for independent expansion, not as a closed research menu. A new, reset or uncertain receiver gets the existing compact baseline; send only a delta when that receiver's available baseline is known. Do not have each recipient reconstruct or paraphrase the same history.

`frontier_references.py prepare-adoption` and `recover-work` reuse an optional `context_account` association in the existing owner metadata, or an explicit `--account` JSON input selected by that owner. It contains `source` (repository-relative path plus optional section, field or commit), `sha256` of the selected adopted text, optional `incorporates` entries, and explicit `actual_use` paths when the account consumes saved task dependencies. An incorporation entry identifies `path` and `sha256`; for a Batch result it also identifies `attempt`, with the hash over the canonical JSON result. Use an existing source or section, not a new document per task. Incorporation is the owner's professional claim, not a consequence of a recent file edit. Do not replace the last adopted meaning with an interrupted update; retain the proposed change separately. No association is required for a simple task that already has necessary explicit context.

Default CLI output deduplicates identical long text using `delivered_content_ref` JSON pointers within that output. With `--output`, the existing internal record remains available for machine checks and the returned `.delivery.json` file is the input to send. `--raw` is an explicit diagnostic export, not normal context. Do not send a merged old record instead of the fresh delivery file. Full validation bytes are not model instructions and display projections must never be supplied to a checker as complete bindings.

At the final controllable native handoff, `verify-delivery --path <delivery-file>` checks the exact prepared adoption/recovery view and its declared versions. Send that same checked content. It does not intercept host tools, authorize an action or establish semantic correctness; unsupported native paths remain advisory. Keep original `actual_use` dependencies and correction relationships when copying or renaming a view. For exact omitted evidence, use its retained snapshot and digest (`read-evidence --path <saved-locator-json>` verifies it); ordinary file reading remains available for new discovery. Never silently replace an identified historical version with newer bytes. Missing evidence limits the affected use, not unrelated work.

Portable sessions reuse `update.context` through the existing Coordinator return. `update.context_unchanged` can incorporate specific no-change returns without rewriting that understanding. Unincorporated returns remain visible, and participating dispatch paths verify the final request. `host context-check` checks a supplied request without claiming optional current-use coverage; `host current-use-check` retains its stricter scoped binding requirement. `host context-read <run> <delivered-sha256> <JSON-pointer>` reads exact evidence while the writer is waiting; `/by_invocation/<id>` directly locates an accepted return even when invocation numbers have gaps. Opaque hosts must establish equivalent access before claiming coverage. A changed unstarted input returns for owner reconciliation; started or uncertain work retains its invocation and effects. No new review or approval stage is introduced.

### Current-use correction

At assignment, adoption, continuation and reuse, compare the actual task and return with the original objective, real constraints and selected commitment. A proposed method, small-change preference or incumbent architecture is a working choice unless its current necessity is established. Correct unsupported restrictions in the outgoing task through their existing owner. Adopt supported findings separately from recommendations; a completed local answer does not establish a wider exclusion or end discovery. Apply [Discovery allocation](learning-loop.md#discovery-allocation) to due inquiry and stopping without another reviewer or routine resolver.

Use the current controlling request or objective, including legitimate user changes, separately from the selected local question. A linked source, matching hash or historical section does not establish that role. Retain the actual source selector and needed qualifications; recover the applicable controlling passage when a local excerpt omits them. A standalone narrow user request needs no invented broader parent. Missing context leaves only the affected comparison unresolved; unrelated authorized work continues.

At consequential adoption, read together the controlling objective, incoming question and completion restrictions, returned decisive reasons and switches, and actual outgoing task. Inspect restrictions that shaped the menu even if absent from the result's rationale. Distinguish user limits, valid experiment requirements and provisional methods: allowing a different architecture is insufficient if every answer must still fit the incumbent's interface or feedback package. A useful bound, rejected premise or changed question can inform allocation before a replacement is complete. Keep a supported focused commitment focused; changing scope stays with its owner.

At existing recovery, recover that relation before classifying inherited work as unchanged. No new result, Selection publication, known finding or previously recognized challenge is needed to reach this check. Reuse sufficient applicable context and continue a valid bounded experiment without a new resolver or record. When an inherited restriction lacks a basis, expose its relevant source in this same turn and correct any established discrepancy before affected future use. This is not a per-edit audit. Use the maintained preparation described in [Current-use correction references](current-use-corrections.md) for adoption and recovery; source presentation aids the owner's judgment rather than certifying it.

Use [Finding effects](finding-effects.md) to decide whether a confirmed finding requires correction before the affected use. A supported limited-impact advisory and unrelated work remain available. Assess consequences across the research commitment, including discovery and allocation, rather than only the next harmless command. For a correction required before use, retain its finding reference, affected work/use, obsolete assignment or dependency, and disposition in existing adopted-work/finding state until valid discharge or explicit transfer. Context refresh, renamed work or omission from a rewritten record does not erase an unresolved association. Do not require unrelated work to construct a correction history.

The owner retains the confirmed finding and original affected objects through the maintained adoption operation before replacing them, then saves and adopts the corrected task and dependencies, including authorized uncommitted changes, or establishes why the finding no longer applies. Required retention is independent of optional native context; a failed required publication leaves affected adoption incomplete and recoverable. Historical observations keep their original identities. Before affected reuse or dispatch, ensure the checked scope matches the actual request and consumes those current adopted objects; a passing report for unrelated work, stale request, matching old hash, nonempty reason or `addressed` label cannot substitute. Correct a downstream restriction in place when investment is unchanged; a material ranking defect uses [Resolver result completion](learning-loop.md#resolver-result-completion). A policy update alone does not invalidate all retained decisions. Helper checks establish object use, not semantic correctness.

For a known applicable correction, use [Current-use correction references](current-use-corrections.md) to retain its association and check the actual task source at supported preparation, reuse and consumption paths. The direct-work owner applies the same check to affected work. This conditional check is not a certificate required of every task; passive native observation remains advisory.

For an existing worker, the follow-up explicitly names and supersedes the old restriction and points to the corrected task. A new document beside conflicting retained instructions is insufficient. Inspect the next substantive action or returned work at the existing handoff. On participating paths, recheck a prepared request when it is consumed; report paths or stages the host cannot expose as unverified rather than enforced. Sending a correction proves delivery at most. Do not interrupt or duplicate an already-started external operation for this check; reconcile its effects first. Ordinary wording changes and implementation progress retain the fast path, while changed constraints, completion conditions or known findings require the same owner to examine their actual scope regardless of `focused` or `continuation` labels.

Professional findings and evidence limits constrain what the work supports. Route recommendations and proposed follow-up procedures are advice: the Coordinator assesses their basis and effect on current work through [Active learning chain](learning-loop.md#active-learning-chain), using [Assurance by consequence](batch-evaluation.md#assurance-by-consequence) for review needs. Writing a report or design does not grant authority to choose a route or impose workflow obligations. Explicit user, verified external and current Workflow requirements retain their original authority when quoted downstream; preserve effects already incurred.

Adopt supported conclusions and unresolved issues under [Finding effects](finding-effects.md), reusing sufficient applicable grounds rather than routinely repeating the technical review. When retained facts settle a classification correction, use [Review applicability and adoption](batch-current.md#review-applicability-and-adoption) directly; return only an unresolved material factual error, applicability gap or technical disagreement to the existing professional owner. The implementation owner handles ordinary limited-impact choices; the Coordinator settles priorities and actual execution requirements within real boundaries. A review's maximum supported use describes its evidence ceiling, and its recovery condition describes the needed correction or evidence. Suggested procedures and implementations do not become requirements merely through adoption.

Apply this distinction when a recommendation would change current work or create a workflow obligation, including route changes, reopening, review requirements and execution gates. For a new or challenged requirement, settle the actual consequence it protects and whether existing evidence or a smaller sufficient action covers the use. Stricter task wording is neither proof of necessity nor proof of a defect. Retain a justified requirement; correct an unsupported one at its owner and affected execution path. A requirement does not justify itself by appearing in an adopted plan. Use existing handoff or adoption work, without sentence-by-sentence approval, a waiver, another review or a separate decision-source record. Material growth in support or delay uses the existing [investment trigger](learning-loop.md#when-to-reconsider-investment), including comparison with other research rather than mandatory simplification first. Workers continue ordinary checks and repairs within scope, report discoveries, and pause only actions affected by a real boundary or unresolved effect.

## Working assignments

W describes the overall design; B pursues one independently judged result; a worker invocation advances a coherent part. Name that part's deliverable or question, working location, write surface, applicable sources, current gap and completion condition. Bound completion to the supported use and actual constraints under [Finding effects](finding-effects.md). Correct an overbroad condition in the same assignment; universal protection needs an applicable requirement, not an author's precaution. A repair names the behavior to restore and its limits rather than copying a suggested implementation or merely requiring a finding to be closed. Reference existing checks or the behavior worth checking. Read limits and versions from their owners; give a new context the base task and current progress. Simple work stays in the existing task description.

For work that could hide a growing repair chain, name the next meaningful observation and a safe point at which autonomous work returns for reassessment. Retain that point with the existing assignment or working plan across invocation, owner and B changes; reissuing the same work does not reset a due question. Ordinary editing and testing before that point remain autonomous. A runtime may claim enforcement only when the selected backend actually returns or stops issuing new Agent actions at that boundary; prompt instructions and a pending flag alone do not establish it. Do not interrupt an already-started uncertain external operation merely to meet a checkpoint.

### Decision policy in the existing resolver path

The existing `frontier_references.py resolver` preparation includes `tools/workflow-harness/decision-policy.md` when that optional repository file is readable. It supplies the current policy text and its identity in the existing assignment, without changing the saved research evidence identity. The same independent resolver applies the guidance during its investment judgment; do not commission a second semantic check. It may include a short `policy_disposition` in its ordinary result: `status` is `addressed`, `not_addressed` or `unavailable`, with a `reason` describing actual handling. This is optional reporting, not another admission form or a pass verdict.

`bind-resolution` records `policy_coverage` alongside the existing result. `policy_status` distinguishes `not_provided`, `current`, `changed` and `unavailable`; `judgment_status` distinguishes explicit handling from `not_reported`. Providing a policy does not establish that the resolver used it. An `addressed` report describes the resolver's handling, not independent proof that its judgment is correct; `current` only means the supplied policy identity still matches. The Coordinator still adopts the substantive result through the existing process. Missing, unreadable or changed guidance, omitted reporting and old records do not block ordinary authorized work or automatically reopen a completed investment judgment. A concrete decision defect uses the existing correction path.

When the same saved decision facts already have a resolution, preparation returns that reference and its `reused_policy_coverage`; it neither rewrites the historical result nor runs the judgment again. Direct calls that omit these preparation and binding entry points remain outside this recorded coverage. The adapter below is optional; no campaign must migrate to its runtime or create another ledger to use this existing resolver path.

In a Codex root task, resolver preparation and binding retain a separate proposal pointer; they never replace current adopted work. In the same Coordinator operation that saves Selection or changes its active work, run `frontier_references.py adopt-work --repo <repo> --path <FRONTIER.md>`. The helper reads the saved `current_state` and selected Batch; a caller's `adopted: true` assertion is insufficient. Direct development without a Batch may use `current_state.work_record` in its existing owner document. No separate Selection document is needed. `--session` selects an explicit root when environment identity is unavailable; children cannot register root context.

Ordinary writes of that selected Batch refresh its association automatically. A direct owner edit is detected as stale at the next participating callback; refresh the association from the saved owner without reopening the judgment. Pause/completion is context, never an instruction to resume. Registration failure retains the underlying work and fails open for ordinary authorized development.

On participating native creation/follow-up calls, the observer retains the actual outgoing input and `tool_use_id`, then associates a matching response with that original invocation. At the existing work return, use `frontier_references.py bind-return --repo <repo> --result <existing-result.json> --call <tool_use_id>` to link the result to those facts, or retain that call and result reference directly in the existing non-JSON return. A confirmed dispatch is not worker completion. Missing or unknown responses stay attempted or uncertain; reconcile the host result before repeating. Unregistered roots, unmapped children and unsupported tool paths are uncovered. For root implementation, use the ordinary produced artifact or `Batch.perform` result; no per-command monitor is required.

At the same handoff/return, the existing owner compares the actual task and result with both the selected commitment and its objective basis. Matching records can all preserve the same wrong scope; an `unchanged` statement or `addressed` policy report is insufficient. ### Working plan

Carry decisive prospective conditions and their implications under [Prospective reasoning](learning-loop.md#prospective-reasoning) in the existing plan or rationale. At the ordinary handoff, return or recovery, use the actual task and returned work, or the artifact and working-plan changes for direct implementation, to check that the selected mechanism and feedback survived. A promise to correct, matching identifiers or a delivered reminder does not establish an actual correction. Reuse the judgment when its basis holds; update affected facts rather than restarting the comparison. These responsibilities apply without a participating Hook or readable receipt, and create no per-command check or new document. Scope a repair's behavior evidence to the stage and path actually observed: a correct proposed task establishes neither dispatch nor completed work. Before calling an affected execution path repaired, inspect its actual task, artifact or result and its use where the claim depends on it, reusing existing coverage. Unobserved stages remain unverified without blocking unrelated work. Conversation content can support an assignment check when a native payload is opaque; it is not native capture.

For work spanning contexts, coordinating dependent parts, or delaying useful feedback until a large implementation is complete, the implementation owner maintains a working plan. Use existing ordinary working material, or one mutable document when none fits. The Coordinator assigns its write surface; the implementation owner writes it directly and integrates contributions. Transfer the same record when ownership changes. Keep this development progress separate from Coordinator-owned Batch definition, status and conclusion; it is not another lifecycle inside `batch.yaml`.

The plan names the current deliverable or question, its prerequisites, progress, feedback to obtain, observed results and remaining work. Preserve the research chain's advantage hypothesis and outstanding target feedback under [Whole-chain investment and target feedback](learning-loop.md#whole-chain-investment-and-target-feedback), including the necessary state for that feedback and any still-applicable reason for delay. For a continuing open objective, retain its reachable next discovery point and prior deferrals under [Discovery allocation](learning-loop.md#discovery-allocation). Link to design and evidence rather than copying them. Keep only near-term work precise; unresolved future work can remain an open question. A narrow integrated behavior is usually a useful slice, but research, prerequisite investigations and tightly coupled changes may need a different shape. Individual steps need not be independently publishable or promise an optimization gain.

Use the plan to dispatch and resume, not merely as a record after the work. The implementation owner selects a coherent step whose needed prerequisites are available. The Coordinator's assignment points to that step and its relevant context instead of redispatching all of W. At the existing handoff and return, compare the actual task/result with the selected mechanism and feedback arrangement, including changed prerequisites that would postpone feedback. Correct an affected assignment through the same owner; do not repeat a full investment comparison for routine steps or create a new review. After obtaining feedback, the owner records the result, updates affected dependencies and splits, replaces, merges or reorders remaining steps before continuing. Investigate a shared cause when successive failures implicate the same connection. Step completion records what was learned or made to work; it is not Batch completion, evidence adoption or a Review gate.

Use ordinary file or task operations and Git retention; require neither a commit per step nor a new task tool, identity or frozen plan. If the plan is missing, establish the next useful step in the same working context and continue. No completeness certificate or user approval is needed for this internal decomposition.

### Integration and continuation

When handing work into a new use, the Coordinator points to the current verification entry point and any uncovered prerequisite under [Reuse working knowledge](batch-current.md#reuse-working-knowledge), not just prior verdicts. The implementation owner connects the affected check to that entry point.

Prefer the existing implementation worker for sustained development and connected parts; a reviewer is not that worker. Keep simple work together, including direct implementation under Permissions above. Split only when dependencies or context load justify it, not by file count. Default to sequential continuation in the same B. The implementation owner connects the resulting work and checks affected seams even when another worker supplies a component. This is ordinary integration, not formal admission or a Campaign conclusion. Reuse sufficient evidence; retain one current writer for shared Batch state without an additional integration role or approval.

Carry the selected whole mechanism and its necessary interactions into the assignment, not only the next editable action. Internal decomposition may refine the sequence without narrowing the promised behavior or requiring each step to produce an independent gain. If an outgoing assignment materially changes the selected mechanism or constraints, update that meaning through its existing owner; do not hide the change as ordinary progress.

An assignment is sufficient when its references support starting the next useful exploration. Unknown benefit, internal methods and unanticipated results are work to investigate, not reasons to demand an exhaustive specification, failure catalogue or test oracle. The implementer may refine internal steps, test methods and error handling from existing goals and evidence. Use [Evidence access](#evidence-access) for related fact-checking.

Refine unadopted technical suggestions in the original professional task. Changes to an adopted design's behavior promise, interfaces, ownership or acceptance go to its design owner; changes to comparator, sampling or interpretation go to the existing measurement owner under [Current Batch measurement](batch-evaluation.md). Updating the affected contract need not reopen investment. Only [When to reconsider investment](learning-loop.md#when-to-reconsider-investment) triggers the resolver; a needed write outside the assigned surface requires coordination, not a new B or automatic user question. Preserve unaffected checks and conclusions; reassess the changed use and pause only dependent work.

A supported opportunity may improve a working solution before it fails. Report its factual basis and possible effect in existing progress or the return, without proving superiority or reopening every alternative. The Coordinator arranges work with the relevant owner, preserving the professional meaning. Reuse sufficient rationale until relevant facts change; neither an invocation return nor this feedback creates a routine comparison or report.

Workers repair ordinary defects. At an invocation return, lead with actual findings, important limits and relevant sources, then the remaining gap and proposed next work; keep detailed process in the original working files. The Coordinator adopts the supported result and selects the next work in one turn, applying [Active learning chain](learning-loop.md#active-learning-chain) before resuming. A repair recommendation does not itself authorize automatic continuation. An internal return is neither a terminal B result nor a request for user permission. A missing assignment file or unbound check does not by itself prevent ordinary development. Review adoption, operational-limit changes and Batch conclusion remain Coordinator-owned.

For Reflection work, use [Research Reflection](learning-loop.md#research-reflection) for the trigger, sufficient inputs, exclusive author and Coordinator content check. For subsequent professional work, pass those sources alongside the current problem and open decisions; Reflection suggestions do not fix the answer.

## Research packet

Name the owning Q or D handle, `research_mode: route_landscape | focused_question`, objective-level question and its intended use, applicable project references, evidence starting points, action window and overall research limits, search stop, assigned evidence section, result-packet path, and completion condition. Distinguish the original objective and real constraints from proposed methods. For discovery, these are enough to begin before a method, final interface, predicted improvement or evaluation protocol is known. Retain a due discovery point and prior deferrals when applicable under [Discovery allocation](learning-loop.md#discovery-allocation). Use [Write and adoption ownership](#write-and-adoption-ownership) for source bindings rather than creating another identity chain.

A route-landscape packet also names comparison dimensions, actual scope and freedoms, repository and retained evidence, relevant Reflection and its original sources, and any reopened exclusion or shared high-consequence assumption. Supply the objective gap and open question, with routes and sources as starting points. Use [Route-landscape synthesis](../../research-frontier/SKILL.md#route-landscape-synthesis), including discovery beyond known questions, without adding fields or a checklist. Contributors may find a different problem model or combined approach without first predicting its effect on the supplied options. Keep prior recommendations distinct from facts under [Scoped evidence reuse](learning-loop.md#scoped-evidence-reuse). Possible findings illustrate learning value, not required answers or a complete future branch tree.

A focused-question packet names the relevant adopted evidence and why a cheaper sufficient observation cannot answer it. The parent answers directly without route synthesis, alternative ranking or specialist fan-out. Its completion answers that question within its supported use; it cannot silently close the broader direction question. Landscape stopping instead follows the remaining learning opportunity and actual action/resource bounds under [Discovery allocation](learning-loop.md#discovery-allocation), without a fixed round cap or first-feasible stopping rule.

### Multidisciplinary research

For open direction formation, the professional research lead briefly identifies relevant disciplines or method families and the different explanations they might offer. Arrange complementary Subagents before a preferred answer hardens; the lead also researches and integrates. For research within a selected direction, retain that question's scope; technical subproblem division is useful there without reopening global exploration. The perspective list is provisional, not a coverage report, fixed team or one-Agent-per-discipline requirement. Engineering and mixed methods qualify; a tightly coupled derivation can stay with one researcher.

Give each contributor the common objective and evaluation meaning, actual constraints, original evidence and current capability gap, plus its proposed perspective. Ask it to reinterpret the whole question, inspect applicable methods and propose mechanisms or representations with material potential. It may challenge the framing or cross disciplinary boundaries. Judge methods, sources and reasoning rather than role titles. Share factual retrieval; different models of the same question are not duplicate tasks.

For example, an open question is: "How can we complete more valid work with the available resources and deadline?" All contributors receive the same facts and may propose a whole direction:

| Perspective | Temporary brief |
|---|---|
| Operations and scheduling | Model resource contention, dependencies and ordering; investigate which allocation or scheduling changes could raise attainable output. |
| Control and decision methods | Examine feedback and uncertainty; investigate when adapting decisions could outperform a fixed arrangement. |
| Algorithms and representation | Examine how the work is represented and computed; investigate reformulation, approximation or reuse that could expand reachable capability. |

These illustrate complementary methods, not a fixed discipline list or team size. "Research route A, accelerate route B, evaluate route C" instead partitions existing answers; it does not by itself provide different ways to understand the problem.

At intake, treat an agent-written route menu or overly narrow reading plan as background when the assigned objective is open. Reframe temporary contributor briefs and follow relevant sources within the same Q, retaining useful findings and the actual objective, limits and write surfaces. Explain the adjustment in existing progress or the synthesis; do not rewrite a frozen assignment or create a correction record. Coordinate an actual scope or permission change under [Evidence access](#evidence-access), while continuing unaffected work. Discussing an unapproved method does not execute it.

Briefs are temporary invocation context, not separate Qs, files, identities or review objects. Share the overall Q limits across contributors and synthesis. Independent first judgments may read relevant evidence; prior rankings are background, not an expected answer. Neither an exclusive contribution nor a new direction is owed by each participant.

Follow [Route-landscape synthesis](../../research-frontier/SKILL.md#route-landscape-synthesis) for comparison, combination and necessary exchange. One final synthesis permits useful intermediate discussion, not fixed debate or review rounds. Proceed with usable findings when a contributor is unavailable; name a missing premise only where it affects the recommendation. Specialists return contributions; the lead alone writes the assigned evidence and result packet. This research organization is not repeated after ordinary T/W progress; use existing owners until relevant new facts reopen the question.

### Research return

The result uses Q or D fields, reports `outcome: completed | blocked | evidence_required`, and names missing evidence when incomplete. Lead with actual findings, important limits and sources; preserve professional rationale, recommendations, material conflicts and transfer limits in this result, with detailed process in the original evidence files. Raw specialist or challenger returns create no project state and are never resolver inputs. Apply the researcher's [Authority](../../research-frontier/SKILL.md#authority): recommendations are advisory input to investment comparison, not adopted choices. Coordinator adoption reuses the synthesis rather than rewriting its ranking or stripping its assumptions. Carry the selected rationale and critical assumptions into the existing design or working assignment; handle them through [Ground consequential design choices](technical-design.md#ground-consequential-design-choices), not a second research report or a requirement to copy the proposed components.

## User-decision packet

Name `decision_kind: tradeoff | permission`, the exact unresolved user-owned decision, evidence, technically eligible alternatives or protected Consequence, scope and cumulative limits, affected work, reconsideration trigger, reserved V path, answer path and completion condition. Apply [User decisions](user-decisions.md); reuse an applicable V and do not delegate a question whose answer is already present.

A tradeoff packet gives the material value differences among technically eligible alternatives. A Permission packet gives the uncovered paid, external, sensitive, irreversible or user-controlled scarce-resource Consequence and the effects of permit, decline or conditions. It does not include packet, target, snapshot, authority, execution or result identities. The worker preserves the exact answer; the Coordinator writes V and resumes internal work.
