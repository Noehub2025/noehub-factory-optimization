# User-facing Handoff

Load this file last, immediately before the final reply. Use only accepted persisted state. This pass formats the return to the user; it does not rerun the recorded-state router, direction resolver, review, or state adoption.

## Completion check

The reply is complete only when a reader can identify, without decoding record identifiers:

1. the current objective and reference point from the Brief, F1, and F3;
2. the concrete work completed since the prior handoff and the strongest supported learning from the latest adopted terminal B and E, or from the Generation Reflection after closeout;
3. the important limit on that learning;
4. the valid objective gap from F6, or why it is `unknown`, plus the nearest decision-changing evidence milestone and any conditional parent milestones;
5. every decision-relevant next candidate from the persisted resolver result and Selection, or the exact event needed to complete that set;
6. the recorded ordering, including the dominance or investment reason, stable operational order when technical evidence does not distinguish eligible options, switching conditions, or an exact decision-preventing blocker;
7. one copyable instruction for each action the user can choose now, with its effect and next stop; and
8. the current Budget and authority boundary and what the Coordinator will do after each instruction.

Read resource availability and permissions from Budget and authority records. Do not reopen lower-level evidence merely to enrich the prose. When F6 is unknown, identify the missing bound or comparison rather than inventing a percentage or treating remaining Budget as distance. Pair every procedural gate with the substantive question it exists to answer.

When no later resolver result or Selection exists because the authorized scope ended, say that direction is not yet resolved. Name the closest substantive decision exposed by adopted evidence, or by the latest Generation Reflection after closeout, and the exact event needed to produce the complete candidate set. Offer a planning-only continuation instruction when it is legal. Do not treat visible candidates as exhaustive or recommended before resolution.

When a persisted resolver result and Selection exist, surface every decision-relevant candidate and its recorded ordering. Label a dominant or best-supported action `Recommended`; retain every material alternative and its switching condition. When technically eligible candidates remain non-dominated, reproduce the [single resolver's](learning-loop.md#integrated-direction-resolver) recorded ordering or its actual blocker, with each alternative's distinguishing purpose, full cost, reachability, authority boundary and switching condition. This return format adds no tie-breaker or missing-rule blocker, and does not delegate technical ranking to the user.

If the current request already supplies a qualifying reopening request, tradeoff answer, exact authorization, or input, apply it through the normal router and gates instead of asking the user to repeat it.

## Authorization question withheld by request

If the current request stops after finding-free `AUTHORIZATION_READY` and explicitly withholds the exact authorization question, do not quote that question or suggest that execution is authorized. Still explain the reviewed target's substantive purpose, what readiness does and does not establish, the nearest objective milestone, and every material choice now available. These may include requesting presentation of the unchanged exact question, keeping the target paused or declining it, and requesting a changed target that will require fresh review. This interaction boundary creates no new lifecycle state or authority.

## Copyable instructions

| No legal user action exists yet | Suggest no command. State the exact evidence, event, worker result, or external change that must occur before the user can decide. | Preserves the blocker and prevents a false continuation path. |

Every authorization suggestion comes from the unchanged reviewed object. Every reopening or recovery instruction names planning-only authority and its next stop. A recommendation grants no authority and creates no record until the user sends it as a current request.
