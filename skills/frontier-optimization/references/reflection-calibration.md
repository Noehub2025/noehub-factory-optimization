# Reflection Calibration Contrasts

Read these contrasts only after drafting the research interpretation. They calibrate evidence use; they are not prose templates. Apply their reasoning to the assigned evidence and vocabulary.

## Whole package and component contribution

Evidence: a valid controlled comparison shows that an intervention containing two behavior-bearing components improves the objective. Implementation evidence shows both pathways exist, but no intervention separates them.

- Weak: both components caused the improvement.
- Also weak: no technical learning is possible without an ablation.
- Calibrated: the whole intervention caused the bounded improvement and both pathways are present. Their individual necessity, dominance, mediation, and numerical contribution remain unresolved. Preserve the supported package-level mechanism; separate components only when a pending research decision depends on that distinction.

## Implemented pathway and performance effect

Evidence: source and behavior checks show that the intended pathway exists and activates under its named conditions. No valid objective comparison has measured its effect.

- Weak: the mechanism improves the objective because its implementation passed.
- Also weak: nothing was learned because performance was not measured.
- Calibrated: the implementation hypothesis is supported at pathway level. The performance hypothesis is unchanged. Later objective evidence must test the predicted effect without repeating the already resolved implementation question.

## Research meaning and a precommitted stop

Evidence: an independent valid confirmation reproduces the direction of an earlier result. The campaign rule requires the same stop action for either legal result.

- Weak: the result passed the confirmation gate, so the campaign closes.
- Calibrated: the repeated result strengthens bounded persistence and reduces the live explanation that the earlier observation depended only on its first measurement instance. It does not add component attribution or transfer evidence. This research interpretation is the same whether later action remains open or the Coordinator applies the precommitted stop; only the operational consequence differs.
