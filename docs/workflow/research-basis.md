# Shared research understanding

Keep the research question connected to observations, prior knowledge,
demonstrated capabilities, proposed mechanisms and unresolved conditions.
Use that account when comparing investments, designing consequential
observations and adopting results. Preserve the original owners of goals,
permissions and task decisions.

The portable [research-basis guide](../../skills/frontier-optimization/references/research-basis.md)
defines the optional structured account, its conditions and relations, a worked
decision trace, publication, selected views and declared-use analysis. Sufficient
existing prose remains supported. Neither form certifies complete research or
creates a readiness gate.

Use Python 3.10 or later with PyYAML for the guide's helper operations.
`adopt-context-account` publishes through the existing owner; `research-view`
produces selected text or a diagram; `research-impact` identifies affected
declared uses. Examples in the Skill guide use a project's `.agents/skills/`
installation. In this distribution, use `skills/`; for a Claude Code project
installation, use `.claude/skills/`.

The [task entry](task-entry.md) can compose the adopted account, current discovery
arrangement, original evidence and associated pending results into actual
direction or design input. Direction and scope retain broad context; a design
or continuation may use consumer-owned `research_use` references. Full sources
remain available. An omitted passage is not evidence that it was considered.

Publish understanding through the existing owner and change the affected
assignment when the conclusion requires it. Preserve historical findings, work
identities, resource use and pauses. A new capability does not authorize the
next experiment, replace the research owner or require rebuilding a campaign.

Separate implemented support, actual receiving behavior, adoption in current
work and demonstrated research benefit. Native transport, unassociated results
and semantic adoption remain outside the entry's mechanical guarantee.
